# Krugerplein research for Sumthing LAN Party

Research date: 30 October 2026. Window: 30 October 2021 through 30 October 2026. This is Krugerplein in Amsterdam's Transvaalbuurt, not Paul Krugerplein in The Hague or Krugerplein in Borgerhout. Publication dates and event dates are separated below. Announcements establish plans, not proof that an event occurred.

## Findings from the last five years

### 1 December 2021 neighbourhood Deelritueel

- Publication: 4 December 2021.
- Event: announced for 21 December 2021, with an afternoon gathering on the square and evening lights in residents' windows.
- Confirmed reporting: neighbourhood wish packages contained a candle, poems and a window poster. Karima's Caravan was a place to collect and help prepare packages.
- Possible fictional game hook: deliver neighbours' wish packages, collect candles and light windows in a timed cooperative challenge.
- Source: Oost Online, [Bewoners Transvaalbuurt vieren het Deelritueel](https://oost-online.nl/het-deelritueel-in-de-transvaalbuurt/).

### 2 July 2022 boules hotspot

- Publication: 17 July 2022.
- Event date: no single event; contemporary account of regular use.
- Confirmed reporting: the square's first boules lane gained players from different backgrounds; a second lane followed. The square is described as a popular place to play or watch in good weather.
- Possible fictional game hook: two playable boules lanes, spectators and returning balls. This supports a social, mixed-age square rather than a map populated only with antagonists.
- Source: Oost Online, [Het begon in de Watergraafsmeer Vijftig jaar later](https://oost-online.nl/het-begon-in-de-watergraafsmeer-vijftig-jaar-later/).

### 3 September 2022 greening and underground works

- Publication: 28 September 2022.
- Event dates: work ongoing at publication; gas-pipe replacement expected at the end of 2022. Do not describe the planned replacement as completed without another source.
- Confirmed reporting: greening was unfinished. Underground pipes limited new tree planting; planters and facade gardens had been installed. Residents maintained the new greenery, and Liander was expected to replace an old cast-iron gas pipe before the remaining greening.
- Possible fictional game hook: carry planters, water neighbourhood gardens, route a furniture delivery around temporary roadworks.
- Source: Oost Online, [Fietsen van postzegelpark naar geveltuin XXL](https://oost-online.nl/fietsen-van-postzegelpark-naar-geveltuin-xxl/).

### 4 April to October 2023 large scaffold letters and public art

- Publication: 19 May 2023.
- Event dates: ten large scaffold letters were reported on the square since early April; programme planned April to October 2023.
- Confirmed reporting: Moving Arts Project's WAARHEDEN HEDEN WAAR used letters forming the word 'de Waarheid', linking art, conversations, performances and local participation.
- Possible fictional game hook: distinctive temporary scenery for a 2023 chapter, carrying letters or collecting lost workshop props. These are a dated installation, not permanent street furniture.
- Source: Oost Online, [De Waarheid op het Krugerplein](https://oost-online.nl/de-waarheid-op-het-krugerplein/).

### 5 February 2024 neighbourhood police profile

- Publication: 5 February 2024.
- Event date: contemporary profile, no single event.
- Confirmed reporting: an interview with neighbourhood officer Dolf Dijkstra describes his work in the Transvaalbuurt and an approach involving conversation with young people, including on Krugerplein.
- Possible fictional game hook: a friendly fictional patrol officer who gives directions, helps organise the moving van or requests a clear walkway. Do not invent misconduct or dialogue attributed to the real named officer.
- Source: Oost Online / Dwars, [Op het Krugerplein met hangjongeren kletsen](https://oost-online.nl/op-het-krugerplein-met-hangjongeren-kletsen/).

### 6 March 2024 collecting neighbourhood stories

- Publication: 22 March 2024.
- Event date: recurring activities, no individual date stated.
- Confirmed reporting: Geheugen van Oost sought Transvaalbuurt stories and held writing and photography activities at Karima's Caravan on Krugerplein.
- Possible fictional game hook: collect photographs or deliver a story/photo to the caravan; a neighbourhood memory wall between game chapters.
- Source: Oost Online, [Geheugen van Oost zoekt verhalen over Transvaalbuurt](https://oost-online.nl/geheugen-van-oost-zoekt-verhalen-over-transvaalbuurt/).

### 7 January 2025 official Caravan event application

- Publication: 23 January 2025, Gemeenteblad 2025 number 27269.
- Application received: 5 January 2025.
- Proposed programme: beginning 22 March 2025; the notice's narrative says through 22 December, while its explicit end-date field says 21 December 2025. Preserve this discrepancy rather than silently selecting one.
- Confirmed notice: application for KARIMA's Caravan near Krugerplein 32A, with Thursday/Friday/Saturday opening, drinks, food, meetings, workshops, performances, exhibitions and markets. Listed maximum attendance: 250. This is an application notice, not confirmation of a granted permit or actual attendance.
- Map value: strongest located public reference found for a caravan placement near number 32A. It does not prove the caravan stood there throughout Sumthing's occupancy; ask Guido to confirm the relevant years and position.
- Possible fictional game hook: coffee delivery, a market day, or workshop preparations.
- Source: [Official application notice PDF](https://www.officielebekendmakingen.nl/gmb-2025-27269.pdf). Search indexing returned the full official notice text; direct opening failed in the research tool. It can also be located by its exact Gemeenteblad identifier on Officiele Bekendmakingen.

### 8 May 2026 Stuck in the Middle opening

- Publication: 11 May 2026.
- Event: opening reported as 'yesterday', therefore 10 May 2026.
- Confirmed reporting: Moving Arts Project's arts festival opened on the square with music, theatre, spoken word and conversations. The organiser quoted in the report estimated around 350 visitors, makers and other attendees.
- Primary organiser corroboration: its visitor page identifies Krugerplein as the festival hub, with a sculpture park, performances and Karima's Caravan serving refreshments.
- Possible fictional game hook: set up a small stage, deliver programme flyers or connect neighbourhood helpers. Do not turn the quoted estimate into a precise attendance count.
- Sources: [Oost Online opening report](https://oost-online.nl/stuck-in-the-middle-zet-tussenmensen-in-het-licht-op-het-krugerplein/), [Moving Arts Project visitor information](https://movingartsproject.nl/stuckinthemiddle/bezoek/).

### 9 August 2026 announced boules tournament

- Publication: 9 August 2026.
- Event: announced for 22 August 2026, with 29 August as a bad-weather fallback. The article is an announcement; the research did not verify which date the tournament actually happened.
- Confirmed announcement: Karima's Caravaan organised a tournament for pairs, including beginners, with music and refreshments.
- Possible fictional game hook: pair up for a short boules errand or retrieve the target ball before the next match.
- Source: Oost Online, [Het legendarische Jeu de boules toernooi strijkt neer op het Krugerplein](https://oost-online.nl/het-legendarische-jeu-de-boules-toernooi-strijkt-neer-op-het-krugerplein/).

### 10 September 2026 fountain switched off early

- Publication and announced action: 2 September 2026.
- Confirmed primary source: the municipality listed Krugerplein among 19 fountains shut down early after elevated E. coli was found in some city fountains. It explicitly did not say the bacterium was detected in every listed fountain. Access and filtration considerations also informed closure.
- Map value: the physical fountain belongs on the map, but a faithful late-2026 depiction should not automatically show running water. Earlier years can be shown separately.
- Possible fictional game hook: a plainly invented maintenance/water-routing task. Do not claim Krugerplein's water tested positive.
- Source: Gemeente Amsterdam, [Negentien fonteinen eerder uitgezet vanwege E coli bacterie](https://www.amsterdam.nl/nieuws/nieuwsoverzicht/fonteinen-eerder-uitgezet/).

## Maps and visual references

- [PDOK Viewer](https://app.pdok.nl/viewer/) provides official aerial-image and map layers. [PDOK's 2026 release](https://www.pdok.nl/-/hoge-resolutie-luchtfoto-2026-nu-beschikbaar-bij-pdok) confirms 2026 RGB high-resolution imagery; [2025 release](https://www.pdok.nl/-/luchtfoto-2025-nu-beschikbaar-bij-pdok?redirect=%2Fnieuws) states previous open imagery remains available. Inspect the desired year, licensing and actual layer availability before choosing.
- [Maps Amsterdam](https://maps.amsterdam.nl/) provides the municipality's map catalogue. Use building footprints and aerial views for real spatial relationships, then translate into an isometric game grid. The research has not traced a survey-grade playable map.
- [Wikimedia Commons Krugerplein category](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Krugerplein,_Amsterdam) and its [fountain category](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Fontein_Krugerplein) provide reference photographs. Check each file's license before reuse; viewing as reference does not grant blanket reuse rights.
- [Public Art Amsterdam](https://publicart.amsterdam/projecten/lady-solid/) explains that Peer Veneman's fountain is the one on Krugerplein. Lady Solid was an alternative design and is now elsewhere; do not put the Lady Solid sculpture on this map.
- The needed user references are office interior sketch, actual entrance, upstairs access, years of occupancy, old furniture photos, caravan location during those years and van loading position. Distinct locations and distances can be stylised for play while preserving the square's real relationships.

## Research boundaries

- No reliable public corroboration was found for the user's personal office encounters, BBQ neighbour, Hedi printing, squatters in the office, theft during the move or a Basnoe cash-stash anecdote. Treat those as user-supplied stories or explicitly fictional mechanics; do not label them as reported facts.
- Searches also surfaced serious violent incidents and unrelated crime reporting. These are not proposed as party-game content and do not establish allegations about named cafe visitors, shop owners, residents or the characters in Guido's anecdotes.
- An 'Oud Nieuws' story published 17 November 2021 concerned historical street-name activism in the 1980s; it was excluded from the five-year event timeline. Its article itself contains conflicting 1982/1987 references.
- A useful boules piece dated 19 September 2021 was outside this strict five-year window and was not counted; the July 2022 story independently supports boules use during the window.
- Search results for The Hague and Borgerhout were discarded. Do not import their police news, braderies, or KrugerKross into Amsterdam.
