## The complete content target

## How we will play

One host opens the game on a laptop connected to the shared screen. Three to ten players join on their phones using the room code or QR code. The host selects each minigame. Points reset for the next game; there is a winner for each minigame and no overall champion.

Allow about five minutes for joining, eight for trivia, nine for characters, eighteen for Krugerplein, seven for restoration, eight for drawing and five for transitions. The host can reveal, advance or finish a game to control the pace.

## Sumthing Sketchy

Provide 15 to 20 concrete prompts that colleagues could draw in 40 to 60 seconds. Reuse people, objects and incidents from the other games. An office chair, a BBQ, a printer emergency and a sofa in a doorway are easier to draw than an abstract company value.
